Fixes #1284, reported by @jonathonbarton.

On a stock level (career on West Coast USA, say), the pause menu's BeamNG.Earth tab showed its full pages and accepted changes, with no visible effect. The side card now shows one orange line while the loaded level isn't the Earth level (and in the main menu's settings page): "Not on the BeamNG.Earth level: graphics and world settings are saved and take effect when you load it."

I kept the tab rather than hiding it. The server, cache, BeamMP and add-on settings still work from anywhere, and changes made there are saved for the next Earth session. If you'd rather hide the tab off the level, that's an easy change.

`offLevel` waits for the first status poll, so the line doesn't flash before the answer comes.
